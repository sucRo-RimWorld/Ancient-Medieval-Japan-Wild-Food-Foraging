## Cross-cutting acceptance cases (also NOT RUN)

- **AC13 — Environment ownership:** Existing Shii and Beech source Defs, normal timber yields, biome climate/ecology remain unmodified. New food-purpose trees/greens/fungi/roots and conditional biome-pool patches belong entirely to Foraging. Foraging requires Environment in game metadata.
- **AC14 — Two actions and saves:** Tree cuts and food gathering are independent; reservation races, cancellation and existing-save migration yield no lost trees, duplicate resource, Def errors or disappearing timber.
- **AC15 — Correctly bounded processing:** Raw ingredient, correctly processed ingredient and full meal are distinct stages when safety requires it. Inedible dangerous young shoots, Fuki alkaloid products, uncooked Shiitake are never silently equivalent to a safe meal.
- **AC16 — Distribution versus habitat:** Approved zone/host/substrate conditions, normal world generation and preexisting-map behavior are tested; `wildPlantRegrowDays` is not assumed equal to individual gathering refresh; no invented commonality or season values.
- **AC17 — Optional profiles:** Baseline Environment with Vanilla first, then separate CCTO, MO and MO+CCTO profiles and any specifically advertised extra-Mod coexistence. **Presence/absence of VV or More Mushrooms cannot veto the twelve adopted species or basic Foraging content.**

## Species-specific planting contracts (also NOT RUN)

**Implementation checkpoint:** As of 2026-10-14, the real [abstract XML templates](../../Defs/ThingDefs_Plants/AMJWF_SowingBases.xml) stage sowing inheritance for future concrete Foraging species. They have no defName and cannot appear in actual RimWorld growing menus. The new [native-XML static guard](../../Tests/Production/test_native_sowing_bases.py) is available but must not be counted as a pawn-job/menu/save test. **AC18–AC22 remain NOT RUN**.



- **AC18 — existing Shii retains Environment sowing:** At Warm in which Shii occurs naturally, after Vanilla `TreeSowing` the original `AMJ_Tree_Shii` remains plantable where Environment permits; before research or in nonnative regions it remains excluded per the Environment regional contract. No Foraging duplicate Shii Def/sow override. Actually complete pawn sow → growth → nuts by independent gather → original wood cut → save/reload; verify normal WoodChain when MO is enabled.
- **AC19 — new Kuri/walnut/Kaya trees:** Each is **Foraging-owned and plantable** only after `TreeSowing` in its eligible native/wild region; no free sowing everywhere. Prove loaded plant inherited/explicit native sow tags, pre/post research grow-zone menu, actual pawn sow Job, growth, nuts/wood paths, save/reload. Planting capability must coexist with natural wild generation and cannot disable native gathering.
- **AC20 — individual mountain-green decisions:** `WF-G01` Warabi and `WF-G02` Zenmai do **not** enter grow-zone planting menus in initial release. `WF-G03` Fuki and `WF-G04` Udo **do** enter a valid existing grow-zone menu and can be sown/grown using native plant mechanics **without inventing new equipment or seed items**; they also remain naturally gatherable. When those plants are harvested, their edible parts, re-growth and safety preparation remain species-specific.
- **AC21 — wild tuber/fungi are not accidentally farmed:** `WF-R01` Jinenjo initially has no grow-zone sowing, instead valid wild underground digging. `WF-F01/F02/F03` are **not sowable** in any grow zone and offer no fungal farming/inoculation; natural appearance requires qualifying habitat/host. Check both missing and completed `TreeSowing` research to catch incorrect inheritance.
- **AC22 — regional gating, cross-Mod and old saves:** Foraging-owned 3 sowable trees cannot appear in planting options outside their accepted natural range; Environment's existing native tree menu remains unchanged when Foraging is added. New planting decisions persist across saves and are tested under Vanilla+Environment, MO, CCTO, and MO+CCTO. No fabricated sow success from menu visibility alone.
