# Ancient & Medieval Japan - Wild Food Foraging — Design

**Author-confirmed name (2026-10-08):** Ancient & Medieval Japan - Wild Food Foraging / 中世日本 - 山野採集  
**Target:** RimWorld 1.6  
**Status:** **12 wild edible natural resources adopted** (initial 8 + 4 evidence-based additions); Shii-specific C# remains inactive. **Actual 1.6 native nonshipping probe Def loading and resolved sowing fields PASS** ([precise evidence](Testing/NativeSowingEvidence-20261014.md)); **NO playable production Mod/plant/item Defs, no approved graphics, nutrition, growth/distribution balance, or completed planting/gathering/saving native acceptance.**

## 1. Purpose and hard boundary

Represent **edible wild plants and forest foods** accessible to people living near woodland, valleys and mountain foothills in ancient-to-medieval Japan, using meaningful settlement food decisions without creating an abstract inexhaustible resource.

**Within scope:** 12 adopted wild-food resources across tree nuts, 山菜, 自然薯 and **wild mushrooms** ([approved list](ResourceAdoption-v1.md)); optional additional species only after their own evidence and adoption review. Seasonal differences are a candidate for an actual survival decision, not yet a numerical production rule.

**Out of scope:** a new animal hunting/trapping/tracking/butchering subsystem; generic fishing, shellfish, seaweed, coastal gathering or shell middens; dedicated food preservation/drying/pickling; grain farming; arbitrary world-map expeditions in the first implementation; placing inexhaustible gathering buildings just to simulate excursions. Vanilla hunting remains unchanged. Edo-specific culture/processing is outside AMJ's target.

**Author-approved change (2026-10-10): AMJ Environment is a required runtime dependency.** Japanese natural vegetation, including the existing `AMJ_Tree_Shii` (スダジイ), is indispensable to the intended medieval-Japan forest gathering experience. The Environment `About/About.xml` packageId is **`sucro.ancientmedievaljapan.environment`**. When Foraging's playable `About/About.xml` is introduced, its `modDependencies` must require that exact packageId and its loading must follow Environment. No playable Foraging About metadata exists yet; this is an approved **design contract**, not a claim that RimWorld dependency metadata has been implemented.

**Dependency direction: Wild Food Foraging → Environment, never the reverse.** **All newly introduced food-purpose wild natural objects**—edible trees, greens, mushrooms and wild roots/tubers—belong to Foraging, including their own PlantDefs or fungal Things, graphics and *Foraging-owned patches* inserting them into Environment biome `wildPlants` pools. Environment owns the underlying climate/terrain/BiomeDefs and its **already implemented baseline trees** (including `AMJ_Tree_Shii`/`AMJ_Tree_Beech`), original forestry/timber and original textures. Foraging adds nut/gathering behavior to these existing trees without duplicating or transferring their original PlantDefs; it does not provide fallback biomes or take ownership of Environment's general scenery. MO, CCTO, DLC and other vegetation Mods remain optional. A required Def does **not** imply Sudajii must grow in every biome or on every map; Environment's regional distribution still governs where it is present.

**Required ecological foundation is distinct from approved food content.** `AMJ_Tree_Shii` and `AMJ_Tree_Beech` already exist in Environment, with Shii selected as the first mechanism trial. The [current adopted list](ResourceAdoption-v1.md) has **12 named food resources**: Shii nuts, Kuri, wild walnut, Kaya nuts, Warabi shoots, Zenmai shoots, Fuki leaf-stalk, wild Udo shoots, Jinenjo root, Hiratake, Matsutake and wild Shiitake **as food resources**. The 2026-10-14 [historical/evidence reevaluation](Research/DeferredFoodReevaluation-20261014.md) explains four additions and their food-safety limits. All new edible species Defs remain Foraging-owned. Beech nuts, other fruit trees and unrelated wild foods are **not** adopted. No harvest values, Defs, timelines or cooking recipes become authorized by adopting the natural items.

## 3. Geography and action interface — confirmed direction

- Work on reachable **cells in the current settlement map**: forest, valley, foothill, natural soil, open cells near rock. A pawn does not need a new mountain-climbing system to gather.
- Mountainous / Large Hills Hilliness is a **candidate factor** for food-resource availability and variety; do not encode a guaranteed numeric hilliness multiplier or fake biome spawn precision before checking the actual RimWorld generator and Environment coexistence.
- Prefer **normal harvest designation and existing Plant harvest jobs** when feasible. New interactions, additional harvest JobDefs or C# are not pre-authorized; prove the missing native behavior first.
- No initial world-map gathering expedition site, abstract production building or infinite spawn mechanic.

## 4. First adopted edible natural-resource set (author-approved scope)

**2026-10-14 amended content scope: twelve wild edible products (initial eight + four evidence-reviewed); adoption is *not* conditional on another Mod's species list.** This supersedes the preliminary species *candidate* inventory for the twelve named products but **does not** approve concrete ThingDefs, XML, harvest quantities, nutrients, cooldowns, season thresholds, art or a playable Mod. The adoption list and historical/biome/food-safety premises are the formal [ResourceAdoption-v1.md](ResourceAdoption-v1.md), which is the only authoritative selected-resource list. **Existing-Mod species overlap is not a criterion for adopting, deferring, or excluding natural foods**: no general external fruit or mushroom Mod represents this project's whole ancient-to-medieval Japanese foraging species list. Similar food Defs elsewhere cannot replace this Foraging-owned content. The old [research candidate inventory](Research/WildResourceSelection-20261014.md) is retained as prior research **not a competing decision**.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forest nuts (4)** | スダジイの実、クリの実、クルミ（オニグルミ系）の実、カヤの実 | Shii: preserve Environment-owned timber/cut. Kuri/wild walnut/Kaya: new Foraging-owned plants and Environment-Biome insertion patches. **Still deferred after reevaluation:** ブナの実、トチの実、ドングリ、コゴミ、タラの芽、クズの根、ナメコ・マイタケ等、ハシバミの実、普通の果樹. Reconsider only their period-use evidence, natural ecology, edible-part/safe processing and this Mod's wild-versus-cultivated scope; **never exclude them because Vanilla, MO, VV, VE or More Mushrooms adds the same plant**. Reasons and conditions are in the adoption list. Dangerous unprocessed bracken/horse chestnut or random unidentified mushrooms are **not approved as safe edible products**.

**Native planting XML groundwork (2026-10-14, scope-limited):** After direct source examination of Vanilla `PlantBase`/`TreeBase` and Environment's regional tree research rules, [NativeSowingEntryDecision](Research/NativeSowingEntryDecision.json) records a scoped `use_as_is` choice rather than creating a growing/farming C# system. Actual [abstract Def templates](../Defs/ThingDefs_Plants/AMJWF_SowingBases.xml) now encode (a) 3 future Foraging nut trees inheriting `TreeBase` **without overriding** `TreeSowing`, `mustBeWildToSow`, existing wood harvest, (b) 2 future sowable Fuki/Udo native `Ground` plants, (c) 6 other non-sowable wild source templates. Existing Environment-owned Shii has **no new XML**. The eleven species are **abstract XML only** (`Abstract=True`, no `defName`), not spawnable, plantable or gatherable yet. No corresponding edible output/graphics/habitat commonality/season/yields have been invented; no RimWorld native loaded-menu or pawn sow tests completed. [Actual XML source contract](../Tests/Production/test_native_sowing_bases.py) guards this stage. This exception to the general implementation HOLD applies **only to abstract native XML inheritance**, not production food content or playable activation.

**Species-by-species planting policy (2026-10-14 author approval):** **Wild-foraging ownership does not imply a blanket no-sowing restriction.** The [Planting section of AcquisitionDesign](AcquisitionDesign.md#11-植栽の種別仕様2026-10-14承認) and [ResourceAcquisitionMatrix](ResourceAcquisitionMatrix.json) are the authoritative individual sowability specification. Environment's existing **Shii** keeps its exact region/research-based tree sowing contract, without Foraging overriding the PlantDef. New Foraging **Kuri, walnut and Kaya** trees are also plantable through the native TreeSowing research gate only in allowed naturally occurring regions. **Fuki and Udo** may additionally be planted using existing grow zones while remaining naturally occurring wild food. **Warabi/Zenmai and Jinenjo** are initially wild-foraging-only, rather than inventing fern spore/special root crop sowing systems. **Hiratake/Matsutake/Shiitake** are not sowable or intentionally cultivable (no fungus beds, log inoculation or farm facilities). Sowing permission does not change selected edible parts, tree wood yields, habitats, optional external-Mod adoption independence or the food safety/processing rules. This is **formal design only**: sowable menu, research unlock, regional gating and genuine planting/growth are not implemented or native-tested.

**12-resource acquisition classification (2026-10-14):** [AcquisitionDesign](AcquisitionDesign.md) and [the source-indexed matrix](ResourceAcquisitionMatrix.json) classify every adopted food product as (a) standing-tree **secondary nut picking** (4), (b) shoot/stalk collection with rhizome/stock recovery to verify (4), (c) **wild underground dig** (1), or (d) naturally occurring **fungal fruiting-body collection** (3). The acquisition *category* and food-safety/habitat constraints are selected for design; the specific XML versus C# mechanism, resource numbers, season windows, work ticks, wild-plant commonality and product graphic remain **unapproved and not implemented**. Forest habitat names by themselves are not sufficient for pine-root Matsutake or dead-hardwood Shiitake/Hiratake. Environment current Cool includes generic `Plant_TreePine` but no AMJE Akamatsu PlantDef; **no automatic Matsutake spawn** is authorized until a verified host/proxy and proximity rule exist. Foraging owns all new edible PlantDefs and any Biome pool patches, existing Shii's wood and growth remain Environment-owned. [AC01–AC17](Testing/ResourceAcquisitionContract.md) are resource-level acceptance tests; **ALL NOT RUN**, distinct from the existing Shii-specific F01–F14.

**Ownership unchanged:** Environment keeps its **existing** Shii/Beech/tree PlantDefs, growth/wood and landscape/climate/biomes. **New food-purpose** wild plants/trees/fungi, their food, source images, optional Environment-Biome spawn patches and gather behavior belong to Foraging. Foraging → Environment is the only required dependency; neither MO, VV nor More Mushrooms is mandatory. The adopted species list is **not** a permission to duplicate workshop Mod assets or alter Environment's existing vegetation.

Existing AMJE `AMJ_Tree_Shii` and `AMJ_Tree_Beech` are currently **wood-producing trees**. Their `harvestYield=42/40` represent timber, **not** food. The new adopted nut products' edible outputs, their season/count and plant/cut flows must be specified independently.

## 5. Prior technical research: tree-nut collection without cutting trees (deferred after natural-resource selection)

**Order correction (2026-10-14):** Resource selection **has now produced the above twelve-item scope**; the Shii-only mechanism must not define the whole Mod. Classify adopted sources into regular plant harvest, standing-tree nut gather, wild root digging and natural fungal harvest **by actual need**, after confirming each historical food-safety and habitat rule. Keep the unactivated Shii C# scaffold inert until its exact first nut product, work amount and repeat constraints are defined and tested; its existence does not justify copying the same Job for mushrooms or wild greens.

**1.6 code inspection result (2026-10-10):** The 1.6 public decompiled `PlantCollected` path already supports a **non-destroying ordinary harvest** when `harvestAfterGrowth > 0`, but the plant's **primary harvestedThingDef is shared by harvest and cutting**. `JobDriver_PlantCut` also explicitly destroys its plant target. Thus merely switching the existing Environment `AMJ_Tree_Shii` to a fruit output, making its existing wood yield repeatable, or attaching an unqualified dual-output extension does **not** establish **separate edible-nut picking while preserving woodcut yields**. The actual MO 1.6 fruit-tree XML demonstrates regrowing primary fruit harvesting, **not** a separate timber output: apple/lemon/mulberry have only primary fruit (20/12/6), `harvestAfterGrowth=0.30`, and no timber-yield extension; mulberry's additional product is silkworm. MO's WoodChain setting targets `WoodLog` primary yields rather than these fruit Defs. The source therefore **predicts fruit rather than timber from mature fruit-tree cutting**, although the actual loaded MO cut/drop result has not been executed. This does not provide the required Shii dual-use timber contract. [MO 1.6 source audit](Research/ExistingModAudit.md#2026-10-14--author-supplied-mo-16-fruit-tree-felling-path-source-only). Read [actual source evidence and restrictions](Research/ExistingModAudit.md#2026-10-10--16-native-harvestwoodcut-source-audit-code-level-not-runtime-pass).

The remaining technical question is whether a **separate tree-preserving nut collection action, reusable for more than one eligible species**, can use native ThingComp/Job capabilities or established small existing mechanics while leaving Environment's original tree planting, growth, timber harvestedThingDef, wood cutting and MO substitution untouched.

**Independent 2026-10-14 fruit-tree corroboration:** VV - New Harvest: Trees' author confirms ordinary fruit picking leaves the tree alive for renewed harvest, and describes its implementation as basic XML only; a third-party 1.6 VGP loaded-error log quotes apple/peach/cherry fruit-primary XML with `harvestAfterGrowth=0.30`. These are orchard/fruit-primary precedents **not an inspected independent second nut output on wood-primary Shii**. See [direct-source and author statements audit](Research/AdditionalFruitTreeModAudit-20261014.md#2026-10-14--primary-sourceloaded-log-follow-up-for-vv-and-vgp). The VV Trees author declares **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**; no copying/modification of upstream artwork/content assets into AMJ is approved. The first-stage technical necessity assessment stays on *preserving the exact existing Environment wood behavior*, not adding an equivalent fruit orchard. Test the common mechanics first against Sudajii, then evaluate applicability to existing Buna and any independently approved future tree (including Kuri); do not treat the Sudajii proof as clearance for other species or their food yields. Research alternate native/VE/non-VE actual implementations and then compare a narrow XML/patch route against minimal justified custom behavior. Do not add an independent Job/C# or gameplay Def before the full Project-format prior-art implementation decision.

**New non-VE distinction (2026-10-14):** [More Tree Products](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3016879545) adds acorns/other byproducts without replacing original tree wood, but does so through a **destruction-loot framework**; it cannot serve the requirement to pick nuts while the tree survives. The current [Expanded Woodworking 2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3576156542) offers MO/VV wood-processing compatibility but has no verified independent living-tree nut collection. These join fruit-primary renewable trees as separate prior-art *categories* in [the owner comparison](Research/AdditionalFruitTreeModAudit-20261014.md#2026-10-14--additional-direct-non-ve-alternatives-tree-destruction-loot-and-wood-processing). A **distinct tree-preserving gathering action with independent finite renewal/save state** is a technical investigation option, not an approved Job, C# class, quantity, or gameplay design. Existing Shii tree-primary timber settings remain untouched; no oak/acorn feature is approved by More Tree Products' precedent.

**Mechanism source finding (2026-10-14):** [Vivi Race 1.6](Research/TreeNutMechanismAudit-20261014.md#2026-10-14--directly-inspected-existing-independent-living-plant-gather-implementation) has an actual **separate JobDriver** targeting a still-living `Plant`, plus a save-serialized gather cooldown. It collects resin from existing trees without using `PlantCollected`, while their ordinary plant harvest remains a different action. It is the strongest observed technical precedent for Shii's separate edible-seed channel. Do **not** depend on Vivi Race: it requires Biotech, Harmony and a large fantasy-race/gather-bench ecosystem; no general source-reuse license was found in the inspected repository tree. Its code and imagery must not be copied.

**Narrow AMJ-side technical direction for further validation (source scaffold exists, not a playable runtime Def/Job yet):** Retain the original Environment `AMJ_Tree_Shii` identity, `TreeBase` wood-primary harvest, `harvestYield=42`, growth, natural distribution and optional MO timber conversion. Explore a new *separate, individually targeted* gathering order/Job, with a small tree-attached saved eligibility/cooldown state. Gathering may yield only the later approved food resource and must never invoke the core wood-harvest/destroy path. Normal cutting must never generate a nut reward. Do not add a gathering bench, load Vivi Race, set resource/season numbers, or add new species. Distinguish **technical feasibility at upstream source level** from an actual loaded AMJ 1.6 proof and validated Project implementation-entry decision.

**Mechanism-only entry decision (2026-10-14; supersedes prior 'no Job/C# yet' text above for this narrow technical scope):** The [Project-format independent-mechanism decision](Research/ShiiMechanismDecision.json) is `status=ready` **only for writing and statically checking original Shii-specific job/state source**; evidence is pinned in [the entry note](Research/ShiiMechanismEntryEvidence-20261014.md). The source at [`Source/AMJWildFoodForaging/`](../Source/AMJWildFoodForaging/README.md) now contains an unactivated tree-targeted JobDriver, designation-filtering WorkGiver and saved per-tree cooldown Comp. This is a code scaffold, **not a playable Mod**: no item, yield, cooldown length, work length, season, graphics, UI, JobDef/WorkGiverDef/DesignationDef, About metadata or assembly is currently supplied. Without product/count/cooldown/work configuration `CanGather()` is always false, intentionally preventing unapproved resource output. Environment's Shii/Beech wood defs and MO WoodChain remain unmodified.

**Native acceptance and remaining gameplay decisions:** The adopted list is fixed above, but actual first item ThingDef/name, food metrics, collection quantity, regeneration/season and UX need accepted owner details before activation. A real 1.6 build plus F01–F14 including native loaded Environment+MO (WoodChain on/off), old saves and cut-vs-gather races are required before declaring functionality. The Project-format validator's structural equivalent has been checked against the decision fields and owner evidence; **its official Python command and any RimWorld runtime have not been executed here**. Other **nonadopted** plant species and additional food recipes remain separate decisions; **Kuri and two wild mushroom species are adopted as resources**, although their Defs, cultivation exclusions, spawn/compatibility and numbers are still not approved.

Determine in order: original native behavior (source checked, loaded game untested) → independent-harvest prior art and loaded Environment+MO coexistence → food-item/patch ownership → real tree survival and **separate** normal timber cutting → seasonal and renewal design → balance/art. No fruit yield, regrowth period or season is fixed.

## 6. Entry tests and release gates

1. **Resource selection completed for this first pass:** [twelve-item adopted-resource list](ResourceAdoption-v1.md) fixes the edible objects/parts, independent of all third-party Mods. Selection rests on historical Japanese use, natural habitat, safe preparation and Foraging's wild-resource scope—not on similar species present in other Mods. Before producing playable Defs, define each item's actual preparation, habitat, nutrition/quantity and harvesting behavior; **do not gate the baseline content implementation on raw VV/More Mushrooms XML or optional coexistence**. Research Vanilla/MO/VE/non-VE source only where needed to choose a new technical harvesting mechanism under the Project implementation-entry rule, or to build **optional compatibility** for users who actually co-install them. Matsutake's suitable host and Warabi/Zenmai food safety remain non-optional ecological/safety requirements. The separate [Shii mechanism-only `status=ready` decision](Research/ShiiMechanismDecision.json) does **not** approve numeric balancing or actual gameplay test completion.
2. The actual native harvest/cut workflow must prove that gathering does **not** consume the tree unintentionally, replace timber yields, or double-reward wood and food. Environment+Vanilla and Environment+MO cases (with optional CCTO profiles) are separate checks; an Environment-free Foraging profile is no longer in scope.
3. **Foraging-owned edible species additions and species-specific sowability** (not a universal ban or permission) and existing-save compatibility adapt the Environment [old-save fruit-tree test specification](https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/blob/main/Docs/GoldenPaths/PlantSowingTests.md): existing-AMJE save → add Foraging and/or upgrade AMJE as applicable → **natural** existing-map appearance for added wild plants → mature real harvest and (where sowable) research-gated sow Job → save/reload, complete logs, ERROR zero. Foraging food extraction from **pre-existing Environment trees** and spawning of **new Foraging-owned edible wild plants in Environment biomes** must be tested as distinct scenarios. Forced-spawn-only tests do not clear natural-spawning claims.
4. Include Environment in **every** runtime profile; distinguish Environment+Vanilla, Environment+MO, Environment+CCTO and Environment+MO+CCTO; do not infer an unrun profile PASS from XML, mocks or historical logs.
5. Approved Japanese historical descriptions before translation, actual accepted artwork and the exact final Steam subscriber payload are separate future gates. No public version, package ID, Workshop ID, runtime PASS or release approval has been assigned.
